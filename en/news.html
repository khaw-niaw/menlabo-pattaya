News

Menu updates, special hours and events

2026.11.11

Open until 5pm on Monday November 16 due to staff training

On Monday, November 16, we will close at 5pm for staff training. Lunch set meals and ramen will be served as usual from 11am to 3pm. We apologize for the inconvenience.

2026.11.11

A new menu is coming in late November

We will introduce a new menu in late November with more dishes that reflect an Osaka izakaya. We will announce the details and start date on this page once confirmed.

2026.10

Our name has changed to Osaka Izakaya MENLABO Sakaba

MENLABO has changed its name to Osaka Izakaya MENLABO Sakaba. Our ramen, including Clear Chicken Shoyu Soba, remains available as before. We also renewed the menu book to match the new name.

2026.06

Lunch service has started

Lunch service is now available from 11am to 3pm with set meals and ramen. The full menu is available from 3pm.

2026.05

A full izakaya menu with ramen to finish

We renewed the menu with Osaka izakaya dishes such as karaage, gyoza and dashi rolled omelet, along with Japanese sake. Ramen remains available to finish your meal.

2024.09

Ramen izakaya service has started in the evening

In addition to ramen, we began evening service with sake and small dishes.

2024.06

Clear Chicken Shoyu Soba is now available

Following Clear Chicken Shio Soba, we began serving Clear Chicken Shoyu Soba.

2024.03

MENLABO has opened in Pattaya

MENLABO, a ramen restaurant, opened on Soi Buakhao in Pattaya. The first menu item was Clear Chicken Shio Soba.
