Osaka Izakaya MENLABO Sakaba

Visit us in Tree Town

Osaka flavors in one place

A Japanese restaurant and Osaka izakaya serving gyoza, karaage and Osaka-style dishes, with ramen to finish. Find us in the black, lantern-lit building inside Tree Town on Soi Buakhao.

Start with Osaka favorites

All prices in Thai baht, VAT included

View the menu

Real Osaka flavor for everyday Pattaya

Enjoy the flavors loved in Osaka right here in Pattaya, in a relaxed setting made for sharing food and time together.

Our izakaya dishes are supervised by Toshiya Nakatani of New Tsurumatsu in Osaka and Yasutaka Taniguchi of Sakiaji, and our ramen by Yuya Tsujita, owner of Men LABO Yuisou in Awaza, Osaka.
