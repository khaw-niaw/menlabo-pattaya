About MENLABO

Threethingsbehindourfood

Osaka flavor

Enjoy dishes loved in Osaka right here in Pattaya, from casual plates to a satisfying meal, all in a relaxed setting.

Made in-house

Our house-made dishes are served simply and honestly, suited to both a solo visit and a table shared with friends.

Japanese sake

Pair Japanese sake with the food, from the first glass to a relaxed Osaka-style evening.

InheritedflavorsandthestoryofMENLABO

Enjoy the flavors loved in Osaka right here in Pattaya, in a relaxed setting made for sharing food and time together.

The LABO in MENLABO means laboratory. No two days are exactly the same.

The menu is supervised by Toshiya Nakatani of New Tsurumatsu in Osaka and Yasutaka Taniguchi of Sakiaji.
